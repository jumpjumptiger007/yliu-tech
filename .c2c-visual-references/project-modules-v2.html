Assistance makes preparation easier while preserving the applicant’s opportunity to review and correct the material before it is sent.

The example assumes submission remains a manual action.

04 Technical

A boundary between preparation and delivery

Preparation produces an inspectable artifact. The workflow stops before delivery, giving the applicant a clear boundary at which to review the output.

This example describes a design relationship, not production behavior or a new canonical Project Story.
